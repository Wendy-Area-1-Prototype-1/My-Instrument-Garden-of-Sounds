1. Project Overview

Project title
Garden of Sounds
Design scenario
Musical Toy for Toddlers

Synopsis

Garden of Sounds is a browser-based musical toy where toddlers explore sound by tapping and moving gardening tools, animals and fruits within an illustrated storybook garden. Each kind of object belongs to its own sound family, and its position in the garden shapes how it sounds, so children can build layered music through play rather than instruction.
